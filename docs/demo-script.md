# NEXORA: 3-Minute Live Hackathon Demo Script

## 0:00 — Opening Statement
> *"Judges, AI has become exceptionally good at thinking. But thinking is useless if the AI cannot reach the human who needs to act. Today, information is scattered across Slack, Telegram, and Email. Humans miss messages, alerts go unacknowledged, and systems stay down. Meet **NEXORA**: an autonomous AI coordination agent powered by Caspian and Featherless AI that turns intelligence into human action."*

---

## 0:20 — Detection & Autonomous AI Reasoning
- **Action**: Click **"SIMULATE CRITICAL INCIDENT"** on the Command Center.
- **Presenter**:
  > *"A critical anomaly strikes our Payment API in India-East: 42% error rate, 8.7s latency, over 18,420 users impacted. Within 3 seconds, Featherless AI classifies this as a P0 incident with 97% confidence and generates incident INC-2026-0042."*

---

## 0:40 — Caspian Reach (Telegram)
- **Visual**: Show the **NEXORA Response Graph** pulsing blue towards Telegram.
- **Presenter**:
  > *"NEXORA looks up our unified human registry, identifies Alex Vance as the Payments lead with a 94% response rate, and reaches him on Telegram through Caspian, starting a 10-second SLA countdown."*

---

## 1:00 — Autonomous Escalation (Timeout)
- **Visual**: Watch the countdown hit 00:00. Telegram turns red (Timeout).
- **Presenter**:
  > *"Alex is unavailable. Traditional alert systems would sit idle. But NEXORA detects the timeout and autonomously escalates to Level 2. It switches channels to Email and contacts Priya Sharma, our Lead Platform SRE."*

---

## 1:25 — Human Response & Memory Search
- **Visual**: Click **"Simulate Human ACK"** (or click Priya Email ACK).
- **Presenter**:
  > *"Priya replies: 'I'm responding. Looking into it now.' NEXORA ingests the response, transfers incident ownership, and searches long-term memory. It finds a similar outage from 18 days ago caused by connection pool exhaustion, giving Priya an instant diagnostic head start."*

---

## 1:45 — Human-in-the-Loop Safety
- **Visual**: The **Human Approval Modal** appears.
- **Presenter**:
  > *"NEXORA recommends rolling back payment deployment #481. But NEXORA never blindly executes dangerous actions. It requests human authorization. We click 'Authorize & Execute'."*

---

## 2:05 — Coordinated Resolution
- **Visual**: Live error rate drops to 0.4%, latency normalizes to 0.32s. Confetti triggers!
- **Presenter**:
  > *"The rollback executes safely. Error rates drop back to 0.4%. We confirm resolution, completing the incident loop."*

---

## 2:20 — Automated AI Postmortem
- **Visual**: Open the **AI Post-Incident Report**.
- **Presenter**:
  > *"NEXORA automatically synthesizes a complete postmortem with root cause analysis, timeline, and preventive actions, exportable in Markdown with one click."*

---

## 2:45 — Analytics & Closing
- **Visual**: Switch to the **Analytics** and **Human Directory** tabs.
- **Presenter**:
  > *"Why does this need Caspian? Because NEXORA isn't useful if it can only think. Caspian gives NEXORA the reach to contact every human, on every channel, with zero delay."*
